FAQ

Questions, answered.

What exactly does riOs do?
riOs lets you pay for your subscriptions months in advance. You choose a subscription and how many months to cover, approve the total once in PayPal, and riOs pays each renewal on its due date until the money runs out.
Does riOs store my card details?
No. You pay inside PayPal's own checkout, and riOs only receives PayPal's confirmation that the payment went through. It never asks for, receives or stores card numbers or your PayPal password.
Can my AI spend my money on its own?
No. Your AI can prepare a payment, but only you can approve it, inside PayPal. Once money is locked, its payee and monthly amount can't change, so even a confused or tricked AI can't send it anywhere else.
Can I get my money back?
Yes. Within 3 days of approving a deposit you can cancel it and get back everything not yet paid out. After that, the money stays locked for your provider. That's the point: it's there when the renewal comes.
What if a renewal payment fails?
Nothing is lost. If PayPal rejects a payout, the money goes straight back to your locked balance and that subscription is paused until you sort it out.
What data does riOs keep?
Only what it needs to pay your renewals: each subscription's name, price, payee and schedule, plus a ledger of PayPal transaction IDs. Your API key isn't stored at all, only a secure fingerprint of it. You can rotate your key any time from the console.
Is this real money yet?
Not yet. riOs currently runs on PayPal's sandbox, so every transaction is a test transaction. Live payments are next.
Which AI assistants work with riOs?
Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and any app that supports MCP, the open standard for connecting AI to real tools. Setup takes about a minute. See the docs.
Does riOs charge a fee?
No. riOs is free while it's in beta.
